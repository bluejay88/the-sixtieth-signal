Another said:

The important thing was not that experts agreed.

They asked what evidence would distinguish explanations.

After the seminar, Imani and Vaziri walked outside.

Imani did not answer. Vaziri continued.

“If the evidence crosses your threshold, you have to cross with it.”

That was harder than skepticism.

Skepticism protected Imani from embarrassment. Revision required vulnerability. They turned. Cassian opened it.

The Antarctic sequence contained redundant positions that could predict missing values if the correct grouping was used.

If the sexagesimal parse was right, three damaged sections should reconstruct to specific numbers.

If wrong, they would not. Imani leaned over the console.

“Can we verify?”

Pike’s voice came from the door.

“The older image is lower resolution but predates the damage visible in your current scan.”

Cassian felt his heartbeat increase. They had a prediction. And an independent earlier state. No mythology could help now. No resemblance. No interpretation.

Either the missing values matched or they did not. Imani looked at Cassian.

“Freeze the prediction.”

He did. Cryptographic hash. Timestamp. No edits. Pike connected the old image.

LOAM was not allowed to see it yet.

A human technician extracted the relevant values according to a preregistered process. First section. Predicted: 12. Observed: 12. Nico stopped breathing. Second. Predicted: 30. Observed: 30. Imani said nothing. Third. Predicted: 6. The technician zoomed. Noise. Ambiguous.

Cassian felt relief and disappointment at once.

Then a second imaging channel resolved the mark. Observed: 6. Nobody spoke.

LOAM received the values only after verification.

CHECKSUM PARSE SUPPORTED.

Imani sat down. Nico whispered, “So.” She looked at him. He thought better of it.

For the first time, sixty was not a coincidence they had found after the fact.

“We have a machine.”

She wrote a sexagesimal value on the transparent wall. 1,24,51,10. Nico stared.

“That looks like four separate numbers.”

“In modern notation, yes. In a place-value sexagesimal system, position matters.”

She walked them through powers of sixty, not because Cassian needed the arithmetic, but because she wanted the room to feel how naturally a base system changes what looks simple.

Cassian saw the design advantage immediately.

Sixty tolerated thirds. Quarters. Fifths. Sixths. Tenths. Twelfths.

A civilization dividing cycles, angles, rations, time intervals, or astronomical observations could do a great deal without recurring fractions.

“So it isn’t mystical,” Nico said.

“Correct.”

“It’s convenient.”

“Extremely.”

“That makes it more likely to be reused.”
