“Replication.”

“Living populations?”

“Maybe. Independent ancient individuals first.”

The three graves gave them exactly three samples. Different preservation quality. Different ages.

The youngest individual - Nara's possible temporal neighborhood, though nobody called it that - produced the strongest signal.

The second produced a weaker related pattern. The third failed. Nico looked disappointed. Quispe looked relieved.

“Good.”

“Why good?”

“Because identical perfection would smell like contamination or story.”

They tested laboratory blanks. Negative. Independent extraction facility.

Same partial signal in two individuals.

Then Quispe did something Cassian did not expect.

She asked LOAM to search for known human analogues first, not archive matches. Human biology.

It found regulatory systems with superficially similar spacing relationships.

The alien hypothesis weakened. Imani smiled. Nico groaned.

Then LOAM controlled for those biological structures and a residual remained. Smaller. Harder. More credible. Quispe looked at the screen.

“That is the part I care about.”

Cassian understood again. Good evidence became less dramatic as it improved.

The mystery was shrinking into a form that could survive expertise.

“That still proves nothing.”

But for the first time, her voice sounded like she was saying it to herself.
