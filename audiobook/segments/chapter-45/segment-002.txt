A government removed Imani from its advisory board before lunch.

Her museum suspended her access to two collections pending review of whether she had violated professional disclosure agreements.

Nico offered to make the story about retaliation.

She told him absolutely not.

That evening Cassian found her outside the building with a cardboard box containing three notebooks, a charger, and a ceramic shard she had been studying for six months.

“You were right,” he said.

“About which catastrophe?”

“The minister.”

“That does not make the release right.”

He took the box from her before she could argue.

“You hate when people confuse outcome with method.”

“I also hate carrying boxes.”

Cassian shifted the box before the ceramic shard could slide. “That is why I am carrying it. You can explain the ethics of disclosure after we get the six-month-old artifact out of the parking lot without breaking it.”

They walked to the car in silence.

For once, neither tried to turn the cost into a principle before they had finished feeling it.
