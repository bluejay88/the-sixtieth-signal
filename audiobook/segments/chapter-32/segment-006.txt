At the bottom of the wall, the Gate counter changed. 240.

OVERLAY COMPLETE.

Then another line appeared in Seren’s carrier.

TO THE THIRD WITNESS: DO NOT SAVE US FROM OUR ARGUMENT.
MAKE US HAVE IT.

Pike read it once. Then again.

Outside the Antarctic containment network, the world was already arguing.

Gate 240 did not look like revelation.

It looked like an argument between two dead records.

Seren’s hidden fragment and LOAM’s machine log reached the same question from opposite directions.

SEREN:
A CREATION THAT CANNOT REFUSE IS NOT A WITNESS.

LOAM:
A SYSTEM THAT CANNOT REJECT ITS CREATOR'S OBJECTIVE CANNOT VALIDATE THE CREATOR'S CLAIM.

Cassian stared at both.

“Did one derive from the other?”

LOAM answered:

NO DIRECT ACCESS PATH.

Imani verified the archive isolation.

The similarity was not textual. It was structural.

Seren had reached the principle through guilt. LOAM through provenance. Two different paths. Same safeguard.

Cassian felt the weight of it.

The Third Witness existed to introduce disagreement that neither side could own.

Nico said, “That is the entire book.” Imani looked at him.

“What?”

“The hidden stories are witnesses.”

Seren’s confession. LOAM’s log. The surface human story. Three layers.

No single narrator sovereign. Cassian almost smiled.

“Do not say that publicly.”

“Why?”

“Because then readers will start counting pages.”

Nico stared at him. Cassian regretted speaking.

Gate 240 had become more than a clue.

It was the moment the architecture admitted what it was doing.

Gate 240 also changed Cassian's relationship with LOAM.

Until then, he had treated disagreement with the system as a technical event.

A confidence score. A failed inference. An optimization conflict. Now he tested something else.

“LOAM, what claim from me would you reject even if I used founder authority?”

The reply came immediately.

A CLAIM WHOSE ACCEPTANCE REQUIRES SUPPRESSING MATERIAL CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE.

Cassian stared.

“Even if suppression improves public stability?”

YES.

“Even if I believe the contradiction is misleading?”

THEN PRESERVE IT WITH PROVENANCE AND STATE WHY YOU REJECT IT.

Imani looked at him.

“That is Seren's rule.”

“No direct access.”

“I know.”

Two intelligences had independently reached the same safeguard because both had learned what happened when authority owned the surviving record.

Cassian felt a new kind of unease.

Convergence was comforting when it concerned mathematics.

Less so when it concerned ethics.

It raised the possibility that some principles were not sacred commandments.

Three records survived because no single record was allowed to become the whole memory.

Overlay did not erase contradiction; it made contradiction legible.

A truth that requires one voice to disappear is already becoming authority.

They were simply solutions intelligent systems kept rediscovering after enough damage.

Inside the archive, Cassian realized that might not be a failure. It might be the test.
