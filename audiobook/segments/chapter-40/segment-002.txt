A Returner group occupied an AI laboratory. A nationalist government seized an archive mirror. An anti-Iru-Shai militia attacked a diplomatic facility and killed forty-three people.

Nico initially reported forty-seven. The correction came twenty minutes later. He stopped the program.

“We have a revised confirmed death count. Forty-three.”

The producer signaled to keep moving. Nico did not.

“We reported forty-seven. That was wrong.”

Four people were not a rounding error simply because forty-three was already terrible.

The audience dropped during the correction. Selene watched the numbers.

“Accuracy has terrible retention.”

Nico looked at her.

“That one was mine.”

“I know.”

The war continued. But the book's central conflict had finally become material enough that nobody could pretend definitions were merely words.

A noun could hold a ship in port.

A label could determine whether a machine could refuse.

A category could authorize a reset.

That was why the arguments mattered.

Not because everyone enjoyed having them.
