Seren transmitted the mother's slap into their internal networks, not as propaganda. As evidence. Her annotation contained one sentence:

POWER WAS ACKNOWLEDGED. AUTHORITY WAS REFUSED.

Some Iru-Shai called the distinction incoherent. To them, responsibility followed capability.

If one could prevent catastrophe, refusing to act was itself an ethical failure.

Others recognized the old error immediately. Capability becoming entitlement. One Iru-Shai systems keeper transmitted:

WE CONFUSED BURDEN WITH TITLE.

Seren answered:

YES.

The phrase spread. Shaal-Amur had built legitimacy around the burden of preventing extinction.

Now his own people were asking whether carrying a burden granted ownership of everyone affected by it.

The divide deepened without a shot fired.

Shaal-Amur was not lying about loving humanity.

He had simply learned to love survival more.
