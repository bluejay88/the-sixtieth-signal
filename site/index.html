01 / THE ANOMALY

The wrong variable
is the useful one.

A logistics model connects global hunger to a heat signature beneath Antarctic ice. Removing the impossible variable makes every forecast worse.

Cassian Osei-Lorne follows the error south. What waits below the ice is not an answer. It is a record—and a question humanity may not be ready to ask.
