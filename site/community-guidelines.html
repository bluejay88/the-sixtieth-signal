Community Guidelines

The Circle is intended for thoughtful discussion among readers. Participate as yourself, respect spoiler gates, and disagree with ideas without attacking people.

Not permitted

Harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, doxxing, spam, impersonation, automated posting, piracy, or attempts to defeat access controls are not permitted. Do not publish other people's private information.

Spoilers and discovery

Use the appropriate Gate for spoiler discussion. Do not post complete answer keys or deliberately remove another reader's opportunity to discover the Signal architecture.

Moderation

Content or access may be limited when needed for safety, integrity, rights protection, or community health. These guidelines will be expanded before the Circle opens.
